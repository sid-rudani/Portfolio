Bumble Bee

June 2026 - July 2026 | Python, PyTorch, Google Colab

A from-scratch decoder-only Transformer language model with a character-level tokenizer.

  • Implemented a GPT-style decoder-only Transformer from scratch, including masked multi-head self-attention, positional embeddings, feed-forward networks, residual connections, and autoregressive text generation.
  • Built a complete training pipeline with character-level vocabulary creation, causal language modeling objective, mini-batch training, AdamW optimization, and checkpoint-based inference.
